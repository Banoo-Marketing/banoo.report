# Kill Rules

Kill discipline is not optional. Something must be killed or paused every week — even when things are "working."

## Kill Criteria (Any of these triggers a kill review)

### Automatic Kill
- ROI score < 6/10
- No revenue movement in 30 days (for active initiatives)
- No revenue path in 90 days (for speculative initiatives)
- Time cost > 10 hrs/week with ROI < 7

### Strong Kill Signal
- High time cost + low leverage ratio
- No strategic alignment with "AI-First Growth Operator" path
- Decreases sovereignty (adds dependency, complexity, or obligation)
- Manual execution without automation path
- Requires more resources before generating revenue

### Soft Kill Signal
- "Interesting but not urgent" — defer to backlog
- Duplicates another active initiative
- Owner/champion is not Emod and has stalled
- Not in top 3 priorities for 2+ weeks

## Kill Process

1. Name the initiative
2. State the kill reason (required — no reason = no kill log)
3. Estimate hours freed per week
4. Redirect freed capacity to highest-ROI remaining initiative
5. Log in kill_list.json + decisions_log.json

## What Killing Is Not

Killing is not failure. It is:
- Focus discipline
- Capital reallocation
- Constraint respect
- Compounding bias (doing fewer things better)

## Resurrection Rules

A killed initiative can be restarted only if:
- The blocking constraint has changed materially
- It has a clear revenue path in <90 days
- Restarting it doesn't push active initiatives above 3
- A written reason is documented in decisions_log.json

## Kill List Review Schedule

- Weekly: 1 kill minimum (even if things are "working")
- Monthly: review all paused/killed items for potential restart or permanent archive
