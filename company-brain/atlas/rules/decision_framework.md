# Decision Framework

Every major decision must be scored before acting. This prevents reactive pivots and preserves strategic concentration.

## Scoring Matrix

Score each dimension 1–10. Minimum passing score: 35/60.

| Dimension | Weight | Description |
|-----------|--------|-------------|
| Revenue speed | High | Time to first cash: 0–30 days = 10, 30–90 days = 7, 90–180 = 4, 180+ = 1 |
| Scalability | High | 12–36 month upside: exponential = 10, linear = 5, flat = 2 |
| Effort-to-return | High | Time invested vs value generated: high leverage = 10, equal = 5, low ROI = 1 |
| Strategic alignment | Medium | Fits AI-First Growth Operator path: perfect = 10, partial = 5, unrelated = 1 |
| Dependency risk | Medium | How many external dependencies: none = 10, few = 7, many = 3 |
| Sovereignty impact | High | Increases autonomy = 10, neutral = 5, reduces freedom = 1 |

## Hard Rejection Criteria

Reject without scoring if:
- Cash flow delayed >90 days with no bridge
- Requires hiring before any revenue
- Complexity score > 8 with no existing leverage
- Decreases sovereignty without exceptional upside
- Outside AI + marketing + systems + relationships domain

## Common Decision Errors

| Error | Description | Fix |
|-------|-------------|-----|
| Urgency bias | Deciding fast because of FOMO | Score it first. Wait 24h. |
| Complexity underestimation | "It's just a quick..." | Add 3x to effort estimate |
| Revenue optimism | "Once it's built, it'll scale..." | What's revenue in 30 days? |
| Identity override | "This is who I am..." | Apply sovereignty test |
| Validation seeking | "What do you think about this?" | What does the score say? |

## Standing Filter Questions

Before any decision:
1. Does this align with the main machine?
2. What is the 30-day revenue impact?
3. What does this replace or kill?
4. Who executes this — and is it the right person?
5. What is the kill condition if it doesn't work?
